Extension: Fiji Patient Occupation

Official URL: https://core.fhir.health.gov.fj/StructureDefinition/fiji-patient-occupation Version: 0.3.0
Active as of 2026-10-23 Computable Name: FijiPatientOccupation

Copyright/Legal: Distributed under the Creative Commons CC0-1.0 License (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

Describes occupation of a Fiji Patient. This is based on the structure of the NZ FHIR Base extension for ACC Patient Occupation but is separately defined so as to enable a national/regional valueset for occupation codes.

Context of Use

    This extension may be used on the following element(s)

    • Element ID: Patient
